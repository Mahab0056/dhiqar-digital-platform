  Source relations: governorate 3244380; districts 11044685, 11044688, 11044691, 11044692, 11044695, 11044697,
  11044703, 11044705, 11044707, 11044709, 11044713, 11044715; Nasr 11044710, Batha 11044712 and
  Karmat Bani Saad 11044694 (mapped in OSM as sub-districts; the 2024 census counts them as districts, so they are
  cut out of their former parent district). Fetched 2026-10-10 via https://polygons.openstreetmap.fr and
  simplified with shared edges preserved (shapely coverage_simplify, ~400 m tolerance).

Why not geoBoundaries / OCHA COD-AB ADM2: that dataset (CC BY 3.0 IGO, 2021) still has Dhi Qar's five older
districts (Nasiriya, Shatra, Rifai, Suq Al-Shoyokh, Chibayish), which no longer match the 2024 census or the
department registry.

areaKm2 is an approximate area computed from the simplified polygon, not an official figure.

department-buildings.geojson and facilities/*.geojson (+ facilities/index.json) — building footprints, public
facilities (schools, health, police, courts, civil defence, post, municipalities, government offices, universities,
libraries, sports/youth centres) and admin_level=7 sub-districts in Dhi Qar.
  © OpenStreetMap contributors, Open Database License (ODbL 1.0) — https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
  Fetched 2026-10-10 via the Overpass API by scripts/geo/fetch-department-footprints.mjs and
  scripts/geo/fetch-dhiqar-facilities.mjs (cached; re-run with --refresh). Nothing is invented: a department or
  facility without a mapped outline gets a small generic stand-in block, flagged matchQuality "generic" / g=1.
  Only an outline the size of one building is extruded: an office/amenity plot, or a whole walled compound drawn
  as one building=* outline (over 10,000 m², or a plain rectangle over 2,500 m²), is drawn as the site/grounds
  with the buildings mapped inside it. A place mapped twice in OSM (an outline and a point, or two points, of the
  same type within 30 m and with a matching or missing name) is counted and drawn once.
  OSM coverage in Dhi Qar is incomplete, so facility counts are "as mapped", not official totals.
